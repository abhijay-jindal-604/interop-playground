Why interop is hard to see

A failed presentation is one opaque error; the cause sits in a message two hops earlier.

What the verifier says Inji Verify: invalid
ERR_SIGNATURE_VERIFICATION_FAILED
"getX509CertChain(...) must not be null"
→
Where the cause is The walt.id credential, issued two hops earlier, carries no x5c chain in its SD-JWT header. Matched as FP-01 (gap G-55) on the exact exchange.
Speaker: "When a credential fails at a verifier, you get one code. We show you which exchange caused it and why." Source: run 01M5B6KNNY7DC3YK3CR0ETNBN9, report docs/reports/01M5B6KNNY7DC3YK3CR0ETNBN9.md (T-44, live, 2026-10-20).

Fix-it re-runs

We do not only diagnose: we prove the fix by re-running with exactly one change.

→

→
ChildSame run, one knob changed: PASS, classified fixed.

"Changing only waltid.sdjwt.x5c from absent to present makes it pass."
