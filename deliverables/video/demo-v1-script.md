# Demo video v1: script and shot list (2026-10-20)

Narration and timing for the v1 demo video (REQ-39), from plan/13 §2. Captions for the same lines are in [`demo-v1.srt`](demo-v1.srt). The video is assembled from the takes in wave 9.

## Cut and format

- **Silent video with burned-in captions.** No narration audio exists; the captions in [`demo-v1.srt`](demo-v1.srt) are burned into [`demo-v1.mp4`](demo-v1.mp4) (the `.srt` also ships as a side-car). The user may record the voice-over from the Narration column later and replace the captions.
- **Assembled by [`assemble.sh`](assemble.sh)** (`bash deliverables/video/assemble.sh`, ffmpeg only, about 1 min, re-runnable): each take is slowed by at most 2x and its last frame held to the shot length below (Playwright compresses idle time). 1440x900, H.264, 4:20, about 9 MB. If `deliverables/takes/s10-fixit.webm` is re-recorded, re-run the script; shot lengths are the `SHOTS` table in it.
- **S8 is cut** (Inji JVM wallet, JSON-LD and mDoc runs: T-48 and T-49 are cut, T-46 is not part of the video; [`docs/decisions.md`](../../docs/decisions.md), 2026-10-20). Shot numbers keep the plan's S-numbers, so S9 follows S7.
- **S1, S11, S12 are title cards** (plain text on a dark background). S11 names the report and gaps files instead of a screen recording. S12 carries the repository URL and the credits, both `TODO(user)`.

## Recording rules

Browser at 1440 px width, reduced motion off (the diagram animates), local bundle after a clean `scripts/up.sh`. Never on screen: OTPs, holder keys, `.env` values, a LAN IP in the address bar. Narration is a voice-over in sentence case and claims only what the shot shows.
