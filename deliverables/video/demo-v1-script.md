# Demo video v1: script and shot list (2026-10-20)

Narration and timing for the v1 demo video (REQ-39). Captions for the same lines are in [`demo-v1.srt`](demo-v1.srt). The video is assembled from the takes recorded at `82eda1b` (the current UI: the "Document" look, paper and ink with serif titles and a verdict stamp; full-width diagram stage, step rail and inspector below it).

## Cut and format

- **Captions burned in, voice-over added separately.** The captions in [`demo-v1.srt`](demo-v1.srt) are the narration lines below, burned into [`demo-v1.mp4`](demo-v1.mp4) (the `.srt` also ships as a side-car) and timed to the recorded voice-over, which starts 0.35 s into each shot.
- **Assembled by [`assemble.sh`](assemble.sh)** (`bash deliverables/video/assemble.sh`, ffmpeg only, about 1 min, re-runnable): each shot is as long as its spoken line plus a 0.35 s lead and a 0.55 s tail, and every take is recorded to that length, so it plays at 1x (a take may be slowed by at most 2x and its last frame held). 1440x900, H.264, 2:16 (136.1 s), about 9 MB. Shot lengths are the `SHOTS` table in it.
- **Takes are cut by [`scripts/cut-takes.sh`](../../scripts/cut-takes.sh)** from the raw recordings of the live specs (`s02-take.webm`, `s03-s07-slice.webm`, `s09-take.webm`, `s10-take.webm`). Each spec marks its shots with a short magenta strip at the top of the page; the script finds the strips in the video and cuts between them, so no take starts on a blank or half-loaded page and no strip frame is kept. Within a shot each beat is paced to the second the narration reaches it (`at()` in `e2e/live/take.ts`).
- **S8 is cut** (Inji JVM wallet, JSON-LD and mDoc runs: T-48 and T-49 are cut, T-46 is not part of the video; [`docs/decisions.md`](../../docs/decisions.md), 2026-10-20). Shot numbers keep the plan's S-numbers, so S9 follows S7.
- **S1, S11, S12 are title cards** in the product's look: Source Serif 4 title in ink on paper, IBM Plex Sans lines, a short hairline rule (fonts in [`fonts/`](fonts/), SIL OFL 1.1). Captions are IBM Plex Sans on an ink box. S11 names the report and gaps files instead of a screen recording. S12 carries the repository URL and the credited name.

## Recording rules

Browser at 1440 px width, reduced motion off (the diagram animates), local bundle after a clean `scripts/up.sh`. Never on screen: OTPs, holder keys, `.env` values, a LAN IP in the address bar. Narration is a voice-over in sentence case and claims only what the shot shows.
